Stay Safe Online!

The internet is fun and useful, but students must learn how to stay safe online. Let’s learn some important internet safety tips together!

Firewall

A firewall protects computers from dangerous websites and hackers.

Virus Threats

Computer viruses can damage files and slow down computers.

Phishing

Do not click unknown links or share passwords online.

Cyberbullying

Always be kind and respectful when using the internet.

✅ Safe Internet Practices

  • Use strong passwords 🔐
  • Ask parents or teachers before visiting websites 👨‍🏫
  • Do not share personal information 👤
  • Use trusted websites only 🌐
  • Be kind to others online ❤️
🌟 Stay Smart • Stay Safe • Stay Happy Online 🌟
